Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

Developer ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Featherpeak Fury mobile application ("Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is stored and used when you use the Service.

Information stored on your device

Featherpeak Fury is an offline trip planner. Trip plans, gear lists, budgets, preferences and your hiker profile stay on this device. We do not operate an account system and we do not upload that data to a server.

If you add a profile photo, the image is stored only in the app's local storage. Camera and photo-library access are used solely for that photo. You can remove the photo at any time from the profile screen, or erase everything from Settings.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, IP addresses, or location data. The Service does not send notifications and does not include advertising or install-attribution SDKs.

How that information is used

  • Show and calculate your trip plans on this device
  • Compare pack weight against the body weight you entered
  • Display your profile name and photo inside the app

Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Third-Party Services

The Service does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or attribution SDKs. Legal pages (Privacy, Support, FAQ) are bundled inside the app and open without a network connection.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.
